FishDex Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Overview

FishDex is a fishing trip logging application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and password when you create an account.
  • Fishing Data: Rivers, trip logs, catch records, fly patterns, notes, and other fishing-related information you choose to enter.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates when you pin a river location or trip location on the map. This is only collected when you manually place a pin — we do not track your location in the background.
  • Photos: Images you upload for river profiles are stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the FishDex service and its features
  • To sync your data across devices
  • To share fishing data with members of your group (only people you invite)
  • To display weather and river flow conditions based on your river locations

Data Sharing

Your fishing data is shared only with members of your fishing group. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication hosting
  • Open-Meteo: Weather data (we send river coordinates, not personal data)
  • USGS / Colorado DWR / USBR: River flow data (we send coordinates, not personal data)
  • OpenStreetMap / Photon: Map and geocoding services
  • Google Maps: Map display on Android devices
  • Apple Maps: Map display on iOS devices

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Some data is also cached locally on your device for offline access.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you have an active account. You can delete your trip data and notes at any time through the app. To delete your account entirely, contact us at the email below.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data and account
  • Export your data

Children's Privacy

FishDex is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app.
